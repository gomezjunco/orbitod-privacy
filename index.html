Privacy Policy for OrbitOd

Last updated: October 9, 2026

OrbitOd respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and processed when you use the OrbitOd mobile game.

Information We Collect

OrbitOd does not require users to create an account or directly provide personal information in order to play the game.

However, OrbitOd uses third-party services that may automatically collect certain information, including device information, advertising identifiers, IP address, app interactions, diagnostic information, and information related to advertisements.

Advertising

OrbitOd displays advertisements using Google AdMob. This includes banner advertisements and rewarded advertisements that players may choose to watch in exchange for in-game benefits.

Google and its advertising partners may collect and process information in order to provide, measure, and improve advertising services.

For more information about how Google handles information, please visit Google's Privacy & Terms website: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Multiplayer Gameplay

OrbitOd includes online multiplayer functionality. Information necessary for multiplayer gameplay, such as player names, gameplay state, and network communications, may be transmitted between the game and OrbitOd's game servers.

Children's Privacy

OrbitOd does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Third-party services used by the app may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable laws.

Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information associated with the operation of OrbitOd. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Third-Party Services

OrbitOd may use third-party services including Google Play services and Google AdMob. These services operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be published on this page and the "Last updated" date will be revised when appropriate.
